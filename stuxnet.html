The Stuxnet Cyberattack

How the world's first digital weapon sabotaged Iran's nuclear program.

Step 1

Intelligence Gathering

US/Israel study Iranian centrifuges, acquiring samples and supply chain insights to build replicas.

Step 2

Identifying Weakness

Pinpointing the Siemens PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) systems that manage centrifuge operations.

Step 3

Planning Covert Sabotage

Designing malware to subtly alter centrifuge operations (rotor speed) without triggering alarms.

Step 4

Infiltration via Contractors

Infection spreads via infected USBs and laptops, "jumping the air-gap" into the secure Natanz facility.

Step 5

Payload Execution

The malware records 21 seconds of "normal" sensor data, then replays it while manipulating centrifuge speeds.

Step 6

Physical Damage

Centrifuges are sped up and slowed down, causing catastrophic failure, all while operators see "all normal" readings.

Step 7

Global Discovery

Malware leaks globally. Symantec and other researchers identify it as an unprecedentedly advanced weapon using multiple zero-day exploits.

Step 8

Attribution & Aftermath

Widely attributed to the US and Israel ("Operation Olympic Games"). Marks the beginning of the cyber-physical warfare era.
