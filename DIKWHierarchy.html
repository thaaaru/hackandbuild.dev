4 Types of Knowledge

Different ways we store and access what we know.

Explicit

Written down, easy to share. Books, manuals, recipes.

  • Manuals
  • Recipes

Tacit

Experience-based, hard to explain. "Gut feeling" or skill.

  • Riding a bike
  • Muscle memory

Procedural

Step-by-step "how to". Processes and guides.

  • Installation guides
  • GPS directions

Declarative

Facts and definitions. The "What is" knowledge.

  • Paris is in France
  • Water boils at 100°C

What is a Knowledge Gap?

It is the difference between what you know and what you need to know to solve a problem.

Example:

You want to cook Thai food but only know how to make sandwiches. The gap is the spices, techniques, and recipes you haven't learned yet.
