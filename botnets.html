The Botnet Economy

A Look Inside the Business of Cybercrime

Scale & Lifespan

1 Million+ Devices

Large-scale botnets routinely exceed 1 million infected devices. Early Mirai variants hit ~600k, while modern IoT botnets can reach 1.2M.

50-90 Day Lifespan

The average botnet lifespan before disruption. However, P2P (Peer-to-Peer) botnets can persist for years due to harder takedowns.

Only 1-3% Cleaned

Only 1-3% of infected devices are ever cleaned manually. Most disappear as users replace old devices or apply patches accidentally.

Operational Behaviour

Seasonal Spikes

Infections surge during Black Friday, Christmas, and Chinese New Year due to new, cheap IoT devices.

Rapid Exploitation

When a critical CVE (vulnerability) drops, infection campaigns surge within 1-24 hours, often automated.

A/B Testing

Operators launch different payloads on different geographies to measure infection success rates.

Automated Recon

Botnets scan the entire IPv4 space every 10-20 minutes via distributed nodes.

Architecture & Trends

30-40% use P2P C2

Peer-to-Peer Command & Control (C2) is used in 30-40% of botnets. These are significantly harder for CERTs and ISPs to dismantle.

High Resilience

Law enforcement disruptions reduce botnet size temporarily, but resilient operators regrow networks within weeks.

50% C2 Rotation (24-48h)

Half of C2 infrastructure rotates every 1-2 days using fast-flux DNS, compromised WordPress sites, and hacked cloud accounts.

Geographic & Enterprise Trends

Infection Hotspots

Density is highest in regions with low-cost IoT, poor vendor security, and long equipment replacement cycles:

  • South Asia
  • Africa
  • Latin America
  • Eastern Europe

"Silent" Enterprise Infections
